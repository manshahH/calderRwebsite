# Project brief

**Company:** CalderR (calderr.com)
**What:** AI automation agency for property management companies
**Audience:** Directors of Operations, COOs, and Regional Managers at residential property management companies managing 200 to 5000 units, primarily US-based
**Primary CTA across the site:** "Get your free Leasing Performance Audit"
**Brand promise:** Operations-focused AI automation built by people who understand the operational reality of property management
**Tone:** Direct, specific, numbers-driven, confident without being braggy. No corporate fluff. No AI hype words. We talk like operators, not vendors.

# Brand voice rules (enforce these in all copy)

- Lead with specific numbers and outcomes, not adjectives
- No "revolutionary," "cutting-edge," "transformative," "leverage," "synergy," "innovative," "next-generation," "AI-powered" used as a primary descriptor
- No "we're passionate about" or "our mission is to" openers
- Active voice, present tense
- Sentences under 25 words wherever possible
- Skip em dashes; use periods, commas, or parentheses instead
- Operator language: "PMCs," "Director of Ops," "unit count," "rent roll," "leasing inquiries," "maintenance triage," "owner reporting"
- Pricing should be visible (pre-qualifies serious buyers)

# Design system suggestions

**Aesthetic:** Premium SaaS dark theme with operational/technical accents. Think Linear, Vercel, Resend, Stripe Docs. Restrained motion. Generous whitespace. Geometric.

**Color palette suggestion:**
- Background: near-black (#0A0A0A or #0D0D0F)
- Surface: dark gray (#16161A)
- Border: subtle (#2A2A2E)
- Text primary: near-white (#F5F5F5)
- Text secondary: muted gray (#A1A1AA)
- Accent: a single signature color (suggestion: muted electric blue or amber/copper for warmth; avoid neon and avoid generic SaaS purple)
- Success: subtle green for positive metrics
- Warning: subtle amber for issues found in audits

**Typography suggestion:**
- Display: Inter Tight or General Sans (modern, geometric)
- Body: Inter or IBM Plex Sans
- Mono: JetBrains Mono or IBM Plex Mono for numbers, code, and data callouts
- Heavy weight contrast between headlines (700/800) and body (400/500)

**Components and motion:**
- Subtle hover states (color shift, not dramatic transforms)
- Fade-in on scroll, no parallax, no particle effects
- Cards with thin borders, not heavy shadows
- Numerical callouts in mono font, oversized
- Data viz where appropriate (small bar charts, distribution curves)

## Section 2: What the audit covers

**Section headline:** What we measure

Five rows, each with an icon and explanation. Numeric badges (01, 02, 03 etc.) in mono font on the left.

**01 / Response time**
We submit a realistic test inquiry through your website and Zillow listings. We measure how fast you respond, through which channel, and whether you follow up if there's no reply.

**02 / Response quality**
We score the actual response on tone, pre-qualification, tour offer, and next steps. Most operators answer but don't sell.

**03 / Channel coverage**
We check whether you're reachable via SMS, web chat, email, phone, and after hours. The channel a prospect prefers should not determine whether they hear back.

**04 / Listing presence**
We audit your listings across Zillow, Apartments.com, Rent.com, Trulia, and Hotpads. Photos, descriptions, pricing transparency, and contact methods.

**05 / Tenant self-serve**
We check whether tenants and prospects can get answers without waiting for your team. FAQ pages, portals, knowledge bases.

**Body:**
A 4 to 6 page PDF report, branded with your company name on the cover. Overall score out of 100. Section-by-section breakdown with screenshots of your actual responses (or lack of). Comparison to anonymized peer benchmarks. Three specific recommendations.

## Section 5: FAQ

**Section headline:** Common questions

Six expandable items.

**Q: Is this really free?**
Yes. The audit produces real insight whether you ever hire us. We use it as our calling card.

**Q: How do you run the test inquiries?**
We submit through your public contact forms and listings using unique test personas. Within 24 hours, we disclose to your team that the inquiry was part of an industry study. You can request your data to be excluded at any point.

**Q: What do you actually do with my data?**
We use it to produce your branded report and, optionally, to compute anonymized aggregate statistics across operators in your metro. Your specific company is never identified publicly. You can opt out of aggregate use.

**Q: Will you spam me afterward?**
No. We send the report. We follow up once a week later asking if you want to discuss. After that we stop unless you reach out.

**Q: How long does it take?**
Most reports go out within 5 business days. Active audits with response measurement take that long because we wait 72 hours for your team to respond to the test inquiry.

**Q: Who sees the report?**
Only you, unless you choose to share it. We do not publish individual reports.

# Forms specification (for the designer)

All forms across the site share these design rules:
- Single-column layout (never two columns of fields)
- Labels above inputs, not floating
- Required fields marked subtly (small asterisk, not red)
- Submit button full-width on mobile, auto-width on desktop
- Success state: replace form with a clean confirmation message and a soft animation
- Error state: red border on invalid fields, error message below each
- No CAPTCHA visible; use invisible reCAPTCHA or hCaptcha
- All form submissions trigger an email to your team + write to your CRM

# Technical specs (for the designer to coordinate with the engineer)

- Framework: Next.js (App Router) or Astro for the marketing site
- Deployment: Vercel
- CMS for blog: MDX in repo, or Notion-backed via Super, or Sanity if you want a real CMS
- Forms: Formspree or custom webhook to your CRM (Airtable/Notion for now)
- Analytics: Plausible or Fathom
- Calendar booking: Cal.com embedded on contact and audit follow-up pages
- Email transactional: Resend or Postmark
- Performance budget: Lighthouse score 95+ on mobile, sub-1.5 second LCP
- SEO: sitemap.xml, robots.txt, Open Graph tags on every page, structured data for the organization
- Mobile-first responsive
- Dark mode is the default; light mode is optional (skip it for v1)

# A note on what NOT to do (give this to the designer too)

- No particle effects, mouse-tracking animations, or 3D scenes
- No carousel/slider for testimonials or features
- No chatbot widget in the corner of the site
- No "Powered by AI" badges anywhere
- No stock photography (real people, real screenshots, or nothing)
- No more than one font weight per text block
- No drop shadows heavier than 2px
- No gradients on text
- No auto-playing video with sound
- No "live count" or "X people viewing this" elements
- No animated counters that count up when scrolling into view (they're cliche now)

This should be enough for Claude to design and code the entire site in one or two passes. Hand it the brand brief, the design system, the page-by-page content, the form specs, and the technical specs, and tell it to start with the homepage and audit landing page first. Those two pages do 80 percent of the conversion work. The rest can come in subsequent passes.
